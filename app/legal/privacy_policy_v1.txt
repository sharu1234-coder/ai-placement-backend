AI Placement OS — Privacy Policy

Version: 1
Effective date: 2026-08-24
Published by: SGLD Quant Labs

This policy describes what data AI Placement OS ("the App", "we", "us") collects, why, who
processes it, and the rights you have over it. It is written to accurately describe what the
App actually does today, not generic boilerplate — sections that describe planned-but-not-yet-
active features are marked clearly below.

1. Who we are
AI Placement OS is a placement-preparation app (resume analysis, job matching, mock
assessments, mock interviews, and a personalized preparation roadmap) published by SGLD
Quant Labs. Contact us about privacy at privacy@sgldquantlabs.com.

2. Data we collect

Account data: your email address and a securely hashed password (bcrypt — we never store
or can recover your plaintext password).

Profile data: full name, college, graduation year, and target role, plus anything you add
yourself: education history, skills, work experience, projects, and certifications.

Resume data: the resume file you upload (PDF), the text extracted from it, and the
structured analysis produced from that text (skills, education, experience, ATS score,
flagged weak sections).

Job description data: the text of any job description you paste in, plus the requirements
and role match we derive from it against your resume.

Assessment data: your answers, scores, and per-skill results from any practice
assessment you take.

Interview data: the text of your interview answers (typed, or transcribed from your
spoken answer — see "Voice data" below) and the AI-generated evaluation of them
(correctness, clarity, structure, etc.).

Roadmap data: the day-by-day preparation plan generated for you and your progress
against it.

3. Voice data
If you answer an interview question by speaking, your audio is sent for transcription and
the resulting text transcript is what gets stored. The raw audio recording itself is not
saved anywhere, on the device or on our servers, once transcription completes — this is a
deliberate minimization decision, not an oversight.

4. Who processes your data

Our servers: all the data above is stored in our database and, for resume files, in file
storage (a local disk in development; a cloud object-storage bucket — Amazon S3 or an
S3-compatible provider — in production).

Our AI provider: resume text, job description text, and interview answers are sent to an
AI model to generate the analysis, matching, questions, and feedback described above. In
development this runs on our own self-hosted model (Ollama) and never leaves our
infrastructure; in production this is the Anthropic Claude API, operating under
Anthropic's own data-handling terms (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy). We do not
use any other third-party AI provider.

We do not sell your data to anyone, for any purpose.

5. Firebase services
The App uses Firebase (a Google service) for four purposes:

- Push notifications: the App generates a device push token so we can send you
  notifications in the future. This token is not currently sent to or stored on our
  servers.
- Crash and error reporting (Crashlytics): if the App encounters an unexpected error, a
  report is sent to Firebase containing technical details only — the type of error, and
  for network errors, the API path and HTTP status code involved. This report never
  contains your resume content, interview answers, passwords, or authentication tokens.
- Product analytics: Firebase's default, automatic usage analytics (such as app opens and
  screen views) are active. We do not currently send any custom analytics events beyond
  Firebase's own automatic collection.
- App Check: the App asks Firebase to attest that requests come from a genuine installation
  of the App. This check runs on the device only — our backend does not currently verify
  App Check tokens, so this does not yet gate access to your account or data.

See Firebase's own privacy policy for how it handles this data:
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. How long we keep your data
We keep your data for as long as your account exists. There is no automatic expiry.

7. Your rights
You can view and edit your profile, education, skills, experience, projects, and
certifications at any time in the app. You can delete individual resumes and job
descriptions yourself. You can permanently delete your entire account — and everything
listed in section 2 above, without exception — from Settings, by re-entering your
password to confirm. Account deletion is immediate and irreversible: once confirmed, the
data is gone from our systems, not just hidden from you.

8. Security
Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plaintext. Access to the API requires
a signed JWT token. Production deployments are required to run over HTTPS (see our
production readiness checklist — this is enforced at the infrastructure level, not by this
document).

9. Children's privacy
The App is intended for students preparing for job placements (typically college-age and
above) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from
children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy
We version this policy (see the version number at the top). If we make a material change,
we will update the version and effective date here.

11. Contact
Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@sgldquantlabs.com.
